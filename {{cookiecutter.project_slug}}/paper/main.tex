\begin{abstract}

\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Open with the problem and why it matters.

\subsection{Related work}

Survey prior approaches.
Cite papers making contrary claims where you can.

\subsection{Gap}

State what prior work leaves unsolved, then describe the experiments in one paragraph.

\section{Methods}

Define inputs, outputs, and notation precisely.

\section{Results}

Interpret the numbers.
Say what they mean, not only what they are.

\section{Conclusion}

Limitations first, then concrete next steps.
